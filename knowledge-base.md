# Katzrin Knowledge Base / מאגר ידע קצרין
**For the bilingual "Move to Katzrin" family & business chatbot**
Compiled June 2026. Figures are approximate and shift quickly — the town is in a rapid growth phase. Always tell users to confirm current numbers with the municipality, Kupat Cholim, or a local real-estate agent before acting.

> **How to use this file:** Each section is a self-contained knowledge block. Facts are written so they can be served in either English or Hebrew. Hebrew names/terms are kept alongside English so the bot can answer in either language and hand users the exact term they'll need on a government form.

## 1. Overview / סקירה כללית

- **Name:** Katzrin / קצרין (also transliterated Qatzrin/Qasrin). Nicknamed **"The Capital of the Golan" / "בירת הגולן."**
- **Status:** Local council (מועצה מקומית), Northern District. The only urban town in the Golan; the Golan Regional Council (מועצה אזורית גולן) is administered from here (the regional council governs the surrounding kibbutzim/moshavim — a *separate* body from the Katzrin local council).
- **Founded:** July 1977 (decision made under PM Golda Meir's government, 1973; first garin organized 1975). Inaugurated 24 Jan 1978; declared a local council in April 1979. Built next to **Ancient Katzrin**, a Jewish Talmudic-era village destroyed by the 749 CE earthquake.
- **Population:** **8,161** (Israel CBS estimate, April 2026; ~8,126 Israeli citizens), ranked ~200th nationally among local authorities. Second-largest locality in the Golan after Majdal Shams; the largest Israeli town there.
- **Elevation / setting:** ~300–370 m above sea level at the entrance to the Golan plateau. Sea of Galilee (Kinneret) to the south, Mt. Hermon to the north, Upper Galilee to the west. Known for sweeping views and a green, quiet, mountain-town feel.

## 1a. Demographics / דמוגרפיה
*(Fact-checked against Hebrew Wikipedia / Israel CBS, April 2026.)*

- **Total population:** 8,161 (≈8,126 Israeli citizens).
- **Ethnic/religious registration (CBS categories):**
  - Jewish — **83.2%**
  - "Other" (אחרים — mostly non-halachically-Jewish FSU immigrants & their descendants) — **15.8%**
  - Arab-Muslim — 0.8% · Arab-Christian — 0.1% · Druze — 0.1%
- **Former Soviet Union origin:** **More than one-third** of residents are FSU immigrants or their descendants — this is the single most defining demographic fact about the town (drives the large "Other" registration figure, Russian-speaking services, etc.).
- **Age profile (approx.):** 0–14 ≈ 23.6%, 30–44 ≈ 17.9%, 45–59 ≈ 17.3%, 65+ ≈ 15.6%. A family town with a notable senior cohort.
- **Socioeconomic index (CBS 2021):** **Cluster 5 of 10** (national rank ~148) — solid middle, not affluent.
- **Average monthly salary (2022):** ₪8,419 vs. national average ₪11,864.
- **Religious composition:** a genuine mix of **secular (חילוני), religious-Zionist (דתי-לאומי), and Haredi (חרדי)** households living together, plus kibbutz/development-town and oleh backgrounds. English- and Spanish-speaking olim are present alongside the large Russian-speaking community.

## 2. Schools & Education / חינוך
Katzrin's education system is a major selling point — high matriculation (bagrut) rates (~90%) and very high IDF/national-service enlistment. The town markets education as its "growth engine." Crucially, Katzrin offers **both secular (ממלכתי) and religious (ממלכתי-דתי) tracks at every level**, plus Haredi options — so the bot should always ask a family which stream they want.

**The secular vs. religious split — quick guide:**
| Level | Secular / ממלכתי | Religious / ממלכתי-דתי | Haredi / חרדי |
|------|------------------|------------------------|----------------|
| Elementary | **Gamla / גמלא** | **Darkei Noam / דרכי נועם** | Chabad gan; small Haredi frameworks |
| High school | **Nofei Golan / נופי גולן** | **Ulpanat Katzrin–HaGolan** (girls); boys → regional yeshiva options | — |

**Early childhood / גיל הרך**
- Day-care centers (מעונות), private toddler groups (פעוטונים), state secular pre-K & kindergartens (גנים ממלכתיים), state-**religious** kindergartens (ממלכתי-דתי), a **Chabad** multi-age gan (Haredi), and regional therapeutic/special-ed kindergartens.

**Elementary / יסודי (in Katzrin)**
- **Gamla / גמלא** — **secular** state school (mamlachti), grades 1–8, ~460 students. "Amirim" & excellence programs.
- **Darkei Noam / דרכי נועם** — **state-religious** (mamlachti-dati), grades ~1–6, ~220 students; religious values + new-immigrant integration.

**High schools / על-יסודי**
- **Nofei Golan / נופי גולן** — **secular** state regional high school, founded 1985, ~450 students, grades 9–12; shared by the local & regional councils. Uses the "Gevonim" diverse-learning model.
- **Ulpanat Katzrin–HaGolan / אולפנית קצרין-הגולן** — **religious girls'** high school.
- **Branco Weiss / ברנקו וייס** — small, inclusive/personal framework (~86 students per local source) for students who need a more personal setting.

**Post-high-school / religious & pre-army (in Katzrin)**
- **Yeshivat Mor Dror / ישיבת מור דרור** — relocated to Katzrin in 2021.
- **Mechinat Tamir / מכינת תמיר** — national-religious pre-military academy (mechina).

**Higher education / השכלה גבוהה (in Katzrin)**
- **Ohalo Academic College of Education and Sport / המכללה האקדמית אוהלו** — relocated to Katzrin end of 1997 to strengthen Golan settlement; **since 2021 a campus/branch of Tel-Hai Academic College**. ~2,500 students (2017), ~60% in education/teaching. The **only physical-education teacher-training school in northern Israel.** Offers BEd, MEd (education management) and continuing-ed.

**Adult / integration:** Ulpan Hebrew classes (beginner & advanced) several times a year via the community center.

> ⚠️ **Fact-check note:** "Golan School" and "Chispin Yeshiva" are in **Chispin / חיספין** under the Golan *Regional* Council — nearby regional options, **not** in Katzrin town itself. Branco Weiss student counts vary by source (~86–140); state it as "small framework," not a hard number.

## 4. New Construction / בנייה חדשה ושכונות חדשות
Katzrin is in a major expansion phase. In **December 2021 the government approved a demographic-growth program (2022–2025) of roughly 2,500 new housing units** — the authoritative official figure. Promotional materials frame this as "doubling the town" / growing toward ~25,000 residents; treat that as aspirational, not a CBS number. The new quarters are being built via a state tender under the "plan-and-build" (תכנן ובנה) model. Two firms — **Y.D. Barzani (בראזני)** and the **Avney Derech group (קבוצת אבני דרך)** — won the mega-tender to plan, build, and populate a whole new urban quarter.

- **"Nachalim" Quarter / רובע נחלים** — flagship project: ~**1,800 housing units**. Mix of saturated 4–5 story buildings, two-family homes, ground-attached homes (some tourism-combined, e.g., tzimmers), plus commerce, hotels, offices, kindergartens, schools, day-care, mikveh, synagogues, and public/open space.
- **Phase 1:** ~524 units (≈50 ground-attached + the rest up to 5 stories); excavation/shoring permits already issued (work began even during wartime).
- **Two new quarters planned:** **"HaNachalim" / רובע הנחלים (Rova 12)** — ~1,560–1,800 units (sources vary), and **"HaBostan" / רובע הבוסתן (Rova 13)** — a second new residential quarter.
- **"Mehir Matara" (target-price) program / מחיר מטרה:** ~700 units to be sold at subsidized prices — relevant for eligible young families/first-home buyers.
- Earlier approval: a local committee greenlit ~580 units as part of a new quarter (Rova 12 / רובע 12 — "HaRama" by Avney Derech).
- Religious-public-oriented projects also being marketed (e.g., via My-Community / הקהילה שלי).

**Government investment & master plan:** The government allocated **over ₪500 million to Katzrin over four years (2022–2025)** to make it "the most significant city in the region." Planned in the master plan: a **new northern entrance**, public-transport terminal, expanded bike-path network, **Eitan Center renovation**, a new urban market, new soccer field, a **new museum**, hotels/camping, a tech-excellence ("Adom") project, and even a **veterinary school + animal hospital**. Katzrin scored **90 ("high")** on the Interior Ministry's 2025 human-capital/governance index.

**Bot tip:** For target-price eligibility, point users to the Ministry of Construction & Housing lottery system (mishtaknim / "Dira BeHanacha" registration).

### 4a. Self-Build Homes — "Bnei Beitcha" / בנה ביתך
A path many newcomers don't expect: **most homebuilding in the Golan uses the self-build (בנה ביתך) method** — you acquire a plot (often via ISA tender, §18) and build your own house rather than buying a finished unit.
- **Support exists:** the Golan Regional Council + Katzrin Municipality, with the **Israeli Building Center (מרכז הבנייה הישראלי)**, run periodic **"Bnei Beitcha" conferences** and a **~8-session course (subsidized for local residents)** covering planning, financing, green building, thermal insulation, choosing professionals, and build duration — plus consultations with architects, lawyers, and engineers.
- **Bot tip:** when a family asks about housing, offer **three tracks** — (1) buy an existing apartment/house (§3), (2) buy a new/target-price unit (§4), (3) **self-build on a plot** (§4a + §18). Flag the self-build course as a key resource and the absorption dept. (מחלקת קליטה) as a contact.

## 5. Jobs & Employment / תעסוקה ועבודה
Katzrin is the employment hub for the whole central Golan, not just its own residents.

- **Main sectors:** tourism, education, agriculture, free professions, and food/beverage industry.
- **Major employers / flagship industry:** Golan Heights Winery (יקבי רמת הגולן), Mey Eden mineral water bottling plant (מי עדן), Golan Olive Oil Mill (בית הבד), plus the Golan Antiquities Museum and tourism sites.
- **Industrial park / אזור תעשייה קצרין:** large and expanding — ~40 dunams marketed in the last two years, active construction, lot development, infrastructure upgrades. Managed with support from the **Katzrin Development Company**.
- **Research & academia / מחקר ואקדמיה:** Katzrin punches above its weight intellectually — home to the **Shamir Research Institute (מכון שמיר למחקר) / "The Golan Research Institute" (המכון לחקר הגולן)**, founded 1983 by the Ministry of Science, the Golan Regional Council, and the Katzrin Local Council under **University of Haifa** academic supervision. It runs applied research in **agriculture, ecology & environment, sociology & education, and cosmic-radiation monitoring**, and maintains the **Golan Archive** (library + files on Golan history, archaeology, settlement, water, nature). Together with **Ohalo Academic College** (§2), it makes Katzrin a regional research/education center — relevant for academic, scientific, and teaching careers.
- **Employment center / לשכת התעסוקה:** located in the Eitan Commercial Center (מרכז מסחרי איתן). Regional jobs board: **golanjobs.co.il** (מרכז תעסוקה גולן).

## 12. Safety & Security / ביטחון ובטיחות
*Handle this honestly and calmly — it's the #1 unspoken question for anyone considering the Golan.*

- **Border region:** Katzrin sits a few kilometers from the **Syrian border**; the Golan is a frontier area. It is in a **Home Front Command (פיקוד העורף)** zone with a short **siren warning time of ~30 seconds** in the Katzrin area — so quick access to a protected space matters.
- **Protected spaces / מרחבים מוגנים:** Newer homes have a **safe room (ממ"ד)**; buildings may have a floor shelter (**ממ"ק**) or institutional shelter (**ממ"מ**); the town also has **public shelters (מקלטים)**. A municipal emergency operations/control center operates from a shelter in the commercial center. **Bot tip:** advise buyers/renters to confirm a unit has/accesses a ממ"ד or nearby shelter.
- **Apps to install:** the official **Home Front Command app (פיקוד העורף)** and **Red Alert / Tzeva Adom** real-time siren alerts.
- **During escalations** (e.g., the northern situation since Oct 2023), Home Front Command issues activity guidelines (gathering-size limits, schooling only where a protected space is reachable in time). The Golan Regional Council and Katzrin council publish updated instructions.
- **Crime & daily safety:** day-to-day, the Golan is widely regarded as a **low-crime, tight-knit, safe community** — a major draw for families. *(Frame as the lived reputation; specific crime statistics weren't verified here.)*
- **Bottom line for the bot:** be factual about the security reality *and* the strong community resilience; never minimize, never alarm. Point users to פיקוד העורף for current status.

## 13. Climate & Weather / אקלים ומזג אוויר
At ~350 m elevation, Katzrin has a Mediterranean highland climate — four real seasons, cooler and wetter than central Israel.
- **Winter (Dec–Feb):** cold and rainy. Days ~16°C, nights ~4°C. **Annual rainfall ~480 mm**, ~70% falling in these months. **Snow is rare in the town itself** (real snow is on nearby **Mt. Hermon**) but cold snaps and frost happen — **home heating is needed**.
- **Summer:** hot, dry days often **>30°C**, cooler nights (~14°C), **low humidity (~35%)** — pleasant evenings; AC common.
- **Practical:** budget for winter heating; the Golan green season (spring wildflowers, full waterfalls) is a highlight. Live forecasts: AccuWeather/Walla/Maariv "קצרין."

## 14. Nature, Parks & Recreation / טבע, פארקים ופנאי
Katzrin is the **central hub of the Golan's outdoors** — one of its biggest lifestyle draws. Within ~10–40 min:
- **Yehudiya Forest Nature Reserve / שמורת יהודייה** — the **largest reserve in the Golan**; streams (Yehudiya, Zavitan/זוויתן, Meshushim, Dvora), high waterfalls, canyons, natural pools. Wet hikes in summer.
- **Meshushim Pool / בריכת המשושים** — iconic swimming pool (~20×30 m) ringed by **hexagonal basalt columns**; shorter/easier trail, great for families.
- **Zavitan / נחל זוויתן** — beautiful stream with "hexagon" pools and basalt cliffs.
- **Gamla Nature Reserve / שמורת גמלא** — **Israel's highest waterfall (~51 m)**, a **griffon-vulture viewpoint**, and the dramatic ancient Jewish town of Gamla.
- **Mt. Hermon / החרמון** — Israel's **ski resort** & snow play in winter (north of Katzrin).
- **Sea of Galilee (Kinneret) beaches** — ~20–30 min south for swimming, kayaking, watersports.
- **More:** Golan wineries (Golan Heights Winery in town, plus boutique wineries), cycling, horseback riding, jeep/ATV tours, Jordan-river rafting/kayaking, Odem Forest, **Gilgal Refaim** (ancient stone circle), birdwatching.
- In-town recreation (pools, country club, tennis, etc.) — see §11.

## 18. Buying Process & Land Tenure / תהליך רכישה ובעלות על קרקע
A genuinely non-obvious point for newcomers:
- **Most land in Israel (~93%) is state land**, managed by the **Israel Land Authority (רשות מקרקעי ישראל / רמ"י)**. New Katzrin lots and quarters are marketed via **ISA/ИLА tenders (מכרזי רמ"י)**. Buyers typically hold a **long-term lease (חכירה)** rather than outright freehold, though ownership can sometimes be acquired (**הקניית בעלות**).
- **Buying steps:** engage a real-estate **lawyer** (ideally one familiar with Golan/ISA leasing), arrange a **mortgage (משכנתא)**, pay **purchase tax (מס רכישה)** — tiered for a residential apartment (often reduced/zero up to a threshold for a single home), **6%** for bare land — then register the rights (**Tabu / רישום**).
- **Subsidized route:** the **Mehir Matara / מחיר מטרה** target-price units and the national **housing lottery (הגרלות "דירה בהנחה")** for eligible buyers (see §4).
- **Bot tip:** flag the lease-vs-freehold distinction early and recommend a local lawyer; don't quote exact tax owed (depends on buyer status).

## 19. Community Life, Youth & Seniors / קהילה, נוער וגיל הזהב
Beyond synagogues (§11), the social infrastructure newcomers actually use:
- **Community center / מתנ"ס קצרין** — culture, youth & sport center (**2 branches**) running classes (חוגים) and programs for **all ages**, early-childhood through seniors. The hub of town social life.
- **Youth movements / תנועות נוער:** **Bnei Akiva / בני עקיבא** (religious-Zionist, hundreds of local members), **Oz / עוז** (dati-leumi, grades 1–3), **Tzofim / Scouts**, plus a **religious youth club (מועדון נוער דתי)** with a sports hall. Secular and religious options both present.
- **Seniors / אזרחים ותיקים:** an **enriched senior club (מועדון מועשר)** via the matnas, plus "supportive community" (קהילה תומכת) services — relevant given ~15.6% of residents are 65+.
- **Olim communities:** robust **Russian-speaking** services (≈⅓ of the town) and an **Anglo** community supported by Nefesh B'Nefesh coordinators + the municipal absorption dept. and Ulpan (see §2, §10).
- **Social services:** the council's social-services department (katsrin.com) handles welfare, family, disability, and senior support.

## 20. Local Economy, Daily Costs & Information Channels / כלכלה מקומית, עלויות וערוצי מידע
- **Golan economy & terroir:** the region is famous for **wine** (Golan Heights Winery + boutique wineries), **apples & cherries**, **cattle/beef**, **olive oil**, and **Mey Eden** mineral water — plus tourism. Local produce and farm visits are part of life here.

### 20a. Local Food, Wine & Boutique Economy / קולינריה ומוצרים מקומיים
A genuine lifestyle perk and a real income stream (tourism + small producers):
- **Wine:** **Golan Heights Winery / יקבי רמת הגולן** in Katzrin (est. 1983 by 8 kibbutzim/moshavim) — one of Israel's leading wineries, with a visitor center & shop. Plus regional **boutique wineries** — e.g., **Pelter / פלטר** (est. 2002, brothers Tal & Nir Pelter) and its kosher **Matar / מטר** label (2012) and **Pelter Distillery**, **Bazelet HaGolan**, **Odem Mountain**, and more.
- **Cheese, honey & oil:** Golan **boutique dairies** make sheep/goat cheeses (e.g., a local Pecorino served with olive oil & za'atar); **natural honey**, jams, and **olive oil** from Golan presses are widely available — often sold alongside the wineries.
- **Farmers' markets & culinary tourism:** the **"EZ-IZ Market" / שוק עז-איז** Friday farmers' market at **Kibbutz Ein Zivan** (northern Golan) — fruit/veg, Golan wines, honey, oil, cheeses, breads & pastries from local producers; plus **agricultural & culinary tourism** across the Golan (Ein Zivan tourism, tourgolan.org.il).
- **Bot angle:** great for "what's special about living here / lifestyle / food scene / weekend with the family" — and a path for small-business/food entrepreneurs (ties to §6).
- **Daily costs:** groceries via local supermarkets (with the incoming **Shuk Hertzel** mega-store, §6/§7); being in the periphery can add some delivery/logistics premium, but the **periphery income-tax credit (10%/₪80,000, §10)** meaningfully offsets cost of living. **Arnona (municipal property tax)** rates: confirm current tariff with the council.
- **Banking & post:** bank branches and a post office operate in town (confirm which banks for a specific need).
- **How residents actually get information (day-one essentials):**
  - **"Shishi BaGolan" / שישי בגולן** — the local weekly + website: news, **jobs board (דרושים)**, homes/lots for sale & rent, events. (igolan.co.il / shishibagolan.co.il)
  - **Golan-Link portal** + **linkx** app — events, classifieds, second-hand.
  - **Katzrin Local Council** — Facebook, Instagram, YouTube + a municipal **service app** (Arnona, requests, updates). Citizen center **106 / 04-696-2536**.
  - Local **Facebook/WhatsApp groups** where listings, jobs, and recommendations really circulate.

## 21a. Data Confidence & Anti-Hallucination Notes / רמת ודאות
The bot should treat data by confidence tier and **never invent specifics**. When unsure, say so and give the official link.

**High confidence (verified against authoritative HE + EN sources):**

- Schools by stream (Gamla=secular, Darkei Noam & Ulpanat=religious, Nofei Golan=secular); Ohalo College (Tel-Hai branch).

- Government 2021 growth program (~2,500 units); Nachalim quarter (~1,800 units, Barzani + Avney Derech).
- Major employers: Golan Heights Winery, Mey Eden, Golan Olive Oil Mill.
- Security: ~30-sec siren warning time, few km from Syrian border, ממ"ד/shelters, פיקוד העורף zone.
- Climate: ~480 mm rain, winter ~16°/4°C, summer >30°C; snow rare in town (real snow on Hermon).
- Nature hubs: Yehudiya, Meshushim Pool, Zavitan, Gamla (Israel's highest waterfall ~51 m), Hermon, Kinneret.
- Land: most Israeli land is ISA (רמ"י) leasehold; Katzrin lots marketed via ISA tenders.
- Shamir/Golan Research Institute (est. 1983, Univ. of Haifa-affiliated, in Katzrin); Golan Heights Winery (est. 1983) & boutique wineries (Pelter/Matar); Ein Zivan farmers' market.

**Medium confidence (single source / promotional / fast-changing — present with a "verify" caveat):**
- Specific apartment/house **prices** (market moving fast; always link live listings).

**Known conflicts / things to avoid stating as fact:**
